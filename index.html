Hook Effect Verification

Two-Point Dilution Parallelism Test (CLSI EP34)

Sample: Unknown_Sample_1 Neat T/C: 0.0820

In high-dose antigen excess (hook/prozone effect), excess unlabelled antigen blocks sandwich formation at the Test line, leaving only a faint or blank line that mimics a true negative or low concentration.

📋 Verification Protocol
  1. Prepare a 1:10 dilution (10 µL sample + 90 µL running buffer).
  2. Apply to a fresh LFIA test strip and allow migration.
  3. Capture or upload the 1:10 diluted strip image below.

🔬 About RapidQuant

The classical scientific validation of LFIA requires taking photos, manually cropping strips, opening ImageJ to measure band intensities, manually calculating background subtraction and replicate statistics, and fitting 4PL equations.

RapidQuant fully automates this: simply snap or upload strip photos to instantly obtain CLSI EP17-A2 LoD, standard curves, replicate statistics (%CV), and publication-ready figures in seconds.

100% Offline On-Device AI

All AI models are bundled directly inside this app. Works everywhere without Wi-Fi, cellular network, or server connections.
