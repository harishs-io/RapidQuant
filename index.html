🔬 About RapidQuant

RapidQuant is an offline-first point-of-care bioanalytical AI platform that replaces subjective visual inspection of lateral flow immunoassays with laboratory-grade optical densitometry and 4PL concentration quantification (0.1–100 ng/mL).

100% Offline On-Device AI

All AI models are bundled directly inside this app. Works everywhere without Wi-Fi, cellular network, or server connections.

Stage 1 Model: YOLOv8 Strip Crop
Stage 2 Model: YOLOv8 Band Detect
Densitometry: AuNP LSPR (R - G)
Calibration: 4PL / CLSI EP17-A2
Runtime: ONNX WebAssembly
